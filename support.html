Common questions

Why didn't my free storage go up right away?

Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and they keep using space until then. To get the space back now, open the Photos app, go to Albums, then Recently Deleted, and choose Delete All.

Can I get a deleted photo back?

Yes, for 30 days. Open the Photos app, go to Albums, then Recently Deleted, choose the photo and tap Recover.

I use iCloud Photos. What happens when I delete something?

The photo is removed from every device that uses the same iCloud library, and it's also kept in Recently Deleted for 30 days. iCloud's own storage is freed as well, once it leaves Recently Deleted.

Does anything get deleted while I'm swiping?

No. Swiping only builds a pile. Nothing is deleted until you open Review & Shred, tap Delete and confirm on the system prompt. You can tap any photo in the pile to rescue it.

How do I see photos I swiped Keep on again?

On the home screen, scroll down and tap "Show photos I kept again".

Why is a photo in Probably Junk?

Each card shows the reason, such as a receipt, a QR code or a blurry shot. PhotoShred works this out on your device and can get it wrong, so swipe right on anything you want to keep and it won't come back. It skips favourites and photos from the last 30 days, and leaves most photos of people alone. The first scan of a big library takes a while. You can start swiping while it runs, and it carries on where it left off next time.

How does Similar Shots choose the best photo?

It compares resolution and overall image quality on your device and marks its pick with a crown. It's only a suggestion and it can get things wrong, so check each group. Tap any photo to change whether it's kept or shredded, or tap Keep all to leave a group alone.

Which files can PhotoShred clean up?

Only the folder you choose in the Files screen, such as Downloads. iOS doesn't let apps look through your whole device, so files stored inside other apps can't be reached. Files that are only in iCloud are skipped, because they aren't using space on your device.

Can I get a deleted file back?

Usually, yes. Deleted files go to Recently Deleted in the Files app, and they keep using space until you remove them from there. Some locations have no trash. If so, PhotoShred asks you again before deleting permanently, and those files can't be recovered. Deleting a file in iCloud Drive also removes it from your other devices.

What's included for free, and what's in Pro?

Swiping through photos and videos and the shredder are free and unlimited. Similar Shots scans your whole library for free and shows you how much space you could get back. You can clean up 3 groups for free. Listing and previewing the files in a folder is free too. PhotoShred Pro unlocks every group, and lets you shred files and exact copies.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Similar Shots and tap any locked group, or open Files and tap Swipe Through These with Pro, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Is PhotoShred Pro a subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase, so there's nothing to cancel and you're never charged again.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all refunds. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does it scan my videos for lookalikes?

Not yet. Similar Shots looks at photos only. To clear out videos, use the Video Vault deck on the home screen.
