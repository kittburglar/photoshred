Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

The short version: PhotoShred does not collect any information about you. Your photos and files are looked at only on your device and are never uploaded. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy applies to the PhotoShred app for iPhone and iPad, made by Kittiphong Xayasane ("we" or "us").

What we collect

Nothing. PhotoShred does not collect, store or share personal information, and we don't run any servers for it. We can't see your photos and we don't know who uses the app or how.

Your photos and videos

PhotoShred asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos and videos, measure how much space they use, and delete the ones you choose.

  • Comparing photos for Similar Shots, and picking the best shot, happens entirely on your device.
  • Photos and videos are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Photos are deleted only after you confirm, using Apple's Photos system. They move to Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • If you use iCloud Photos, Apple may download a photo from iCloud so the app can show it, and a deletion applies to your other devices that use the same iCloud library. This is handled by Apple.

Your files

If you use the Files feature, PhotoShred reads the folder you choose in Apple's folder picker, and nothing outside it. It uses this to list files, show previews, measure how much space they use and find exact copies.

  • Comparing files happens entirely on your device. Files are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Files are deleted only after you confirm. They move to Recently Deleted in the Files app where the location supports it. If it doesn't, the app asks you again before deleting them permanently.
  • Deleting a file in iCloud Drive also removes it from your other devices. This is handled by Apple.

What stays on your device

PhotoShred remembers a few things so it works the next time you open it:

  • Which photos and files you chose to keep, so they don't come back
  • Which folder you chose, so the app can reopen it
  • Your totals for space freed and items shredded
  • How many free Similar Shots groups you've used

These stay on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

PhotoShred Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment and tells the app whether you've bought Pro. We don't receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

The app doesn't include advertising, analytics or other third-party services.

If you email us

If you contact us for help, we'll use your email address and whatever you choose to include only to reply to you. We won't share it or add you to any mailing list. Ask us at any time to delete your messages.

Children

Because PhotoShred doesn't collect any information, it doesn't collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a future version ever collects information, we'll explain what and why here before that version is released.
